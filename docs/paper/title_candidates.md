## Candidate Titles

1. **Parametric Temporal Leakage in Financial Language Models: Probing Latent Representations Under Causally Symmetric Pretraining**  
   - *Status*: **SELECTED DEFAULT**  
   - *Rationale*: Highly conservative, scientifically precise, directly names the mechanism (parametric temporal leakage), the domain (financial language models), the diagnostic method (probing latent representations), and the core causal methodology (causally symmetric pretraining). Avoids journalistic sensationalism while clearly signaling methodological rigor to ML, NLP, and quantitative finance reviewers.

2. **Beyond Look-Ahead Bias: Measuring Parametric Temporal Leakage Across Representation, Behavior, and Economic Layers in Financial Language Models**  
   - *Status*: Alternative Candidate (Layered Taxonomy Focus)  
   - *Rationale*: Bridges the traditional quantitative finance concept of look-ahead bias with foundation-model parametric leakage; highlights the four-tier layered evaluation stack.

3. **When the Future Is in the Weights: Auditing Parametric Temporal Contamination in Financial Language Encoders**  
   - *Status*: Alternative Candidate (Mechanistic Focus)  
   - *Rationale*: Memorable and clear; emphasizes that temporal contamination is encoded directly within network weights rather than arriving via input runtime features.

4. **Detecting Future Monetary Policy Signals in Language Model Representations: A Causally Symmetric Twin Evaluation**  
   - *Status*: Alternative Candidate (Domain / Empirical Focus)  
   - *Rationale*: Focuses concretely on the empirical target (monetary policy decisions) and the twin-model causal design.

5. **Latent Before Behavioral: Measuring Layered Temporal Leakage in Pretrained Financial Language Models**  
   - *Status*: Alternative Candidate (Conceptual Insight Focus)  
   - *Rationale*: Emphasizes the central conceptual finding that temporal leakage manifests in latent geometry before (and without necessarily) propagating into downstream behavioral or economic outputs.
